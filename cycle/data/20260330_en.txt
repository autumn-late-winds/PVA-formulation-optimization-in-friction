1. R2-01 PVA/Sodium Hyaluronate Chemical Crosslinking Formulation
Composition, 10.00 g
Poly(vinyl alcohol) PVA: 1.20 g
Sodium hyaluronate: 0.05 g
Deionized water: 8.625 g
50 wt% glutaraldehyde solution: 0.10 g
Concentrated hydrochloric acid, 36% to 38%: 0.025 g
Preparation Procedure
First, take about 0.5 g of the total deionized water for this group to pre-wet the sodium hyaluronate, and let it stand at room temperature for 20 to 30 min.
Add the remaining deionized water to a beaker, then add 1.20 g PVA, and heat with stirring at 90 to 95 °C for 2.5 to 3 h until the solution becomes completely transparent.
Cool the system to 55 to 60 °C, then add the pre-wetted sodium hyaluronate and continue stirring for 10 min.
Add 0.10 g of 50 wt% glutaraldehyde solution and stir for 1 min.
Add 0.025 g of concentrated hydrochloric acid, stir rapidly for 20 to 30 s, and immediately cast into a mold.
Place the sample at 50 °C for 2 h to complete the basic chemical crosslinking.
Perform freeze-thaw treatment according to the setting for this group.
For post-treatment, first rinse quickly with deionized water for 5 to 10 min, then equilibrate in fresh deionized water for 1 h before direct testing.
Formulation Purpose

This group is a sodium hyaluronate modified system designed to evaluate the effects of sodium hyaluronate on water retention and lubrication performance.

5. R2-05 PVA/DMSO Chemical Crosslinking Formulation
Composition, 10.00 g
Poly(vinyl alcohol) PVA: 1.20 g
Dimethyl sulfoxide, DMSO: 1.00 g
Deionized water: 7.675 g
50 wt% glutaraldehyde solution: 0.10 g
Concentrated hydrochloric acid, 36% to 38%: 0.025 g
Preparation Procedure
First, pre-mix the DMSO with about 1.0 g of deionized water.
Add the remaining deionized water to a beaker, then add 1.20 g PVA, and heat with stirring at 90 to 95 °C for 2.5 to 3 h until the solution becomes completely transparent.
Cool the system to 55 to 60 °C, then add the DMSO premix and continue stirring for 10 min.
Add 0.10 g of 50 wt% glutaraldehyde solution and stir for 1 min.
Add 0.025 g of concentrated hydrochloric acid, stir rapidly for 20 to 30 s, and immediately cast into a mold.
Place the sample at 50 °C for 2 h to complete the basic chemical crosslinking.
Perform freeze-thaw treatment according to the setting for this group.
For post-treatment, first rinse quickly with deionized water for 5 to 10 min, then equilibrate in fresh deionized water for 1 h before direct testing.
Formulation Purpose

This group is a DMSO modified system intended to evaluate the effect of DMSO as a co-solvent on network formation and final material properties.
