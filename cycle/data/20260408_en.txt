1. R3-01 18% PVA / PDBA / DMSO / Glycerol Formulation
Material Composition, 20.00 g

Polyvinyl alcohol, PVA: 3.60 g
1,4-benzenediboronic acid, PDBA: 0.20 g
Dimethyl sulfoxide, DMSO: 1.20 g
Glycerol: 1.00 g
Deionized water: 14.00 g

Preparation Procedure

Add deionized water to a beaker, then add PVA.
Stir at 90 to 95 °C for 2.5 to 3 h until the solution becomes completely transparent.
In a separate small beaker, add DMSO, then add PDBA, and stir at room temperature to 50 °C to obtain a uniform solution or dispersion as much as possible.
Cool the main PVA solution to 55 to 60 °C.
Add glycerol and stir for 3 to 5 min.
Slowly add the PDBA / DMSO solution and continue stirring for 5 min.
Pour into a mold and briefly degas if necessary.
Perform 1 freeze thaw cycle, namely freezing at -20 °C for 12 h and thawing at 25 °C for 12 h.
After demolding, soak in deionized water for 24 h before testing.

Intended Use

This formulation is used to examine whether glycerol can further improve flexibility and interfacial lubrication on the basis of PDBA dynamic crosslinking.
